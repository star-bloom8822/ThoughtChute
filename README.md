# ThoughtChute
A lightweight website designed to help you process your thoughts and stress. Type out what is bothering you, and watch it physically shred away into digital nothingness.

### 🌟 Key Features
* 🌓 **Dynamic Theme Engine:** Seamlessly toggle between Midnight and Light Mode
* 🎚️ **Interactive Stress Meter:** Tap or drag the stress bar to dynamically reflect and adjust your current mental load.
* 🍃 **Energy Released:** Complete badges like *Stress Fighter*, *Zen Master*, and *Clear Mind* as you successfully clear your mind.
